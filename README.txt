Track - Android app (WebView wrapper, Android 5.0+ incl. Android 11)

Build the APK without installing anything:
1. Create a free account at github.com and make a new repository.
2. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder (including the hidden .github folder).
3. Open the Actions tab, wait for "Build APK" to finish (about 3-5 minutes).
4. Open the finished run, download the "Track-APK" artifact, unzip it.
5. Copy app-debug.apk to your phone and open it. Allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.

Your data is stored on the device inside the app.
